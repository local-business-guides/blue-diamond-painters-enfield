Prepare for a visit from a nearby painter

Take both wide photos and close views of trouble spots. A close-up can show a damaged finish, while a wider picture helps explain where it sits. Include hard-to-reach areas in your notes without climbing up to inspect them yourself.

Have a few practical details ready before the appointment:

  • The rooms, cabinets, or exterior sections you want discussed.
  • Photos of peeling, staining, cracks, or worn areas.
  • Any known leaks or previous repairs.
  • Your preferred timing and dates that won't work.
  • Parking, entry, and pet arrangements.

Leave heavy furniture and attached fixtures in place until you've discussed responsibilities. Ask what you'll need to remove and what the painting crew will handle. That conversation can prevent unnecessary lifting and keep the preparation organized.

Call before buying supplies if you're unsure whether a surface can simply be repainted.
